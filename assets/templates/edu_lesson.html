Lesson 5: Photosynthesis

Biology 101 — Spring Semester

  1. Understand the light-dependent reactions in the thylakoid membrane
  2. Describe the Calvin cycle and carbon fixation process
  3. Identify factors affecting photosynthetic rate (light, CO2, temperature)
  4. Compare C3, C4, and CAM photosynthesis pathways
  5. Analyze experimental data from controlled growth chambers

Key Equation: 6CO2 + 6H2O + light energy → C6H12O6 + 6O2
